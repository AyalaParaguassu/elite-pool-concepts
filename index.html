Fiberglass vs. Concrete

Built Better. Finished Beautifully.

Fiberglass isn't a compromise — it's an upgrade. Smoother surfaces, lower maintenance costs, and a faster installation are just the beginning.

5x Lower Maintenance — Non-porous surface resists algae, reducing the time and chemicals you spend each season.

No Resurfacing Ever — Unlike plaster pools that need resurfacing every 10–15 years, fiberglass gelcoat holds its finish for the life of the pool.

Installed in Days — Factory-manufactured shell arrives ready. Most installs are complete within a week, not months.

Flexible & Durable — Fiberglass flexes with ground movement rather than cracking, making it ideal for most soil conditions.
